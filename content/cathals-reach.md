An Imperial-garrisoned world of timber, logging and backcountry, held by the [[cathals-reach-garrison]] under [[doran-rennick]]. It is the setting of Mission 02.

The locals aren't happy about the occupation, but they aren't terrorised by it either — they treat it more like weather than war. People here credit that peace to [[doran-rennick]], and that peace is exactly why Command wants him dead.

The crew are based at [[redfall-station]].
