[[doran-rennick]]'s second-in-command on [[cathals-reach]]: watchful, blunt and unimpressed by outsiders.

The morning after the brawl at the [[redfall-cantina]], she came to the [[loggers-house]] in person with two troopers, introduced herself as garrison liaison, and asked to speak to [[eleena-daru]] — by name, and alone. News of the brawl had plainly reached her overnight.

She has not drawn a weapon or made a threat. She stands with her hands clasped and lets what she might know do the work. How much she actually knows, and how much she is fishing for, is the question the crew have to answer before they open the door.
