An unmanned freighter, staged as a pirate raider, that [[cassian-vorn]] used as the crew's insertion into [[vannix-station]]. They woke aboard it locked on a collision course with the station's docking arm. [[vesh-talorr]] steered it into a maintenance collar instead, which saved their lives. [[kenno-thorn]] sealed the pressure breach.

Its damaged transponder completed an automated handshake with the station, which brought security to the crash site. The fight with [[kalax]]'s crew, a frag grenade, fire and suppression foam finished it off. It no longer exists.
