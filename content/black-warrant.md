An Imperial gunship that approached [[vannix-station]] during Mission 01, and the true command relay for the [[cresh-spike]] collars. For a moment it identified all five collar-bearers; then [[telsa-brie]] forced its telemetry lock into a false maintenance loop and began redirecting it to the wrong part of the station. The trace was reset, not solved.

It turned out not to matter: the final response that caught the crew near [[bay-nine]] fired without any help from the gunship.
