An unregistered service berth on [[vannix-station]], kept for contractor crews who would rather not appear on the Imperial manifest. [[eleena-daru]] knew the type: low deck, aft, near the reclamation plant, with no transponder logging and an older docking-clamp control that doesn't answer to the central lockdown.

The crew got within sight of its corridor, in a cargo bay at the top of a maintenance ladder, before the [[cresh-spike]] final response dropped all three.
