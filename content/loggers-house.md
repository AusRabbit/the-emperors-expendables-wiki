A borrowed logger's house at the edge of [[redfall-station]], where the crew went to ground after the [[redfall-cantina]] brawl. It smells of woodsmoke and the antiseptic of the night's field dressing. [[vesh-talorr]] is recovering by the hearth.

In grey morning light, [[ilse-wove]] is standing outside the front door with two troopers, asking for [[eleena-daru]].
