The cantina at [[redfall-station]], where off-duty troopers of the [[cathals-reach-garrison]] drank alongside loggers and haulers, a card game ran hot in one corner, and [[bant-corrl]] held court at the longest table.

The bartender was not impressed by [[kenno-thorn]]'s generosity, but he told [[vesh-talorr]] that the town's peace has [[doran-rennick]]'s name on it.

In [[session-3]] it became a three-sided brawl between the [[id-sweep-officers]] and loyal troopers, anti-Imperial locals, and the crew. Two officers died, [[vesh-talorr]] went down in the doorway, and the room was wrecked by the time the constabulary arrived.
