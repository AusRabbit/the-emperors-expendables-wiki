A human hired gun and mercenary soldier who knows Imperial procedure from the inside. He was sentenced after the fallout of an unlawful battlefield order, and for assaulting the officer who tried to make him carry the blame for it. Blunt, direct, violent when required, and deeply suspicious of any command that wants him to be disposable.

## History

- Served with [[doran-rennick]] years ago, before his fall. [[doran-rennick]] was the officer he measured "doing it right" against.
- His full name is something he keeps close. [[elo-kesh]] knew it, which he did not expect.
- Studied his [[cresh-spike]] collar during the fitting: a magnetic latch, a receiver node at the nape, and a recessed technician's service port.
- Made an enemy of [[kalax]].

## On Vannix

Took the [[cautious-nikto|Cautious Nikto]]'s pirate pistol after the wreck. Shielded [[vesh-talorr]] through the checkpoint fight and held the bulkhead doorway under fire until everyone was through, then sealed it. At the debrief he tried "Who's Telsa?" on [[cassian-vorn]], who answered with a voiceprint.

## On Cathal's Reach

Camp boss and muscle for the surveying outfit. Three drinks into a failed charm offensive at the [[redfall-cantina]], he turned a stagger into cover for [[eleena-daru]]. When it broke, he killed the second [[id-sweep-officers|sweep officer]] with a three-shot burst, and afterwards carried [[vesh-talorr]] out on his back.

He has not met [[doran-rennick]] yet.
