A Nikto gunman, one of [[kalax]]'s two associates alongside [[sera]], and part of the competing team [[cassian-vorn]] sent into [[vannix-station]].

When [[kalax]]'s crew went for their weapons in the wreck of the [[mournful-credit]], [[kenno-thorn]] took the fight out of him, and [[eleena-daru]]'s frag grenade killed him — taking some of the false-flag staging material with him. [[kenno-thorn]] kept his pirate pistol.

Deceased.
