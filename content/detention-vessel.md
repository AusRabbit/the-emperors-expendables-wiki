The crew woke here after [[vannix-station]]: cells along a narrow curved corridor, no windows, no chrono, and a deep thrum in the deck that never stopped. [[vesh-talorr]] felt the floor lean, and a distant groan made a guard's eyes flick away like he had heard it before. The guards had been running silent for days.

The crew suspect it is a ship in transit. [[cassian-vorn]] asked whether they knew where they were; they gave him nothing, and he didn't say. It has no name on record — the title here is a placeholder.

The debrief and the Mission 02 orders both happened here.
