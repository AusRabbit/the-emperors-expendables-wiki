[[cassian-vorn]] took custody of a batch of collared prisoners and loaded them aboard the [[mournful-credit]], a freighter staged to look like a pirate raider. The plan was to crash it into [[vannix-station]], and while the station believed it was being raided, the prisoners would recover data, erase evidence and remove witnesses.

## The Wreck

The crash scattered everyone. Two other prisoners, [[kalax]] and [[sera]], became competitors almost at once. A Nikto gunman in the group — [[cautious-nikto|the Cautious Nikto]] — died in the escape from the wreck, caught by [[eleena-daru]]'s frag grenade, and [[kenno-thorn]] came away with his pirate pistol. The [[mournful-credit]] burned behind them, station security started hunting for intruders, and the crew went deeper into the station rather than wait for the cover-up to close around them.

## The Evidence

In [[communications-control]] they found [[telsa-brie]], a junior technician, and a battered astromech called [[rusk]], trying to get proof of an Imperial massacre off the station. The crew froze the outgoing transmission at 91%, but [[rusk]] kept the cleanest copy. A security-droid response forced everyone onto an emergency route, and [[telsa-brie]] became the crew's guide — they were her best chance of keeping the [[vannix-evidence|evidence]] alive.

## What the Collars Are

Through [[telsa-brie]]'s digging, the crew learned the truth about [[cresh-spike]]: an ISB-modified restraint system with tracking, forced compliance, remote stun and a sealed "final response". The collars are batch-linked to a remote command structure. Vannix could mirror their traffic but not remove them, and the true command relay was an approaching gunship, the [[black-warrant]].

## The Core

[[telsa-brie]] got into the [[central-computer-core]] through Station Chief [[arden-vel]], a wounded whistleblower of his own. It turned violent when [[eleena-daru]] took [[telsa-brie]] hostage. [[arden-vel]], two security survivors and the core's security droid went down, and a collapsing gantry buried one of the doors.

[[kalax]] and [[sera]] reappeared mid-fight and accepted a narrow ceasefire after [[eleena-daru]] offered them a path toward collar freedom. [[kalax]] opened a relay panel under the core; the [[black-warrant]] briefly identified all five collar-bearers, and [[telsa-brie]] forced its lock into a false maintenance loop. Then she agreed to steer the gunship to the wrong part of the station.

As she worked, [[eleena-daru]] stepped behind her to finish her. [[sera]], who had twice argued that [[telsa-brie]] was worth keeping, raised her weapon at [[eleena-daru]]. An attempt to make [[kalax]] force his partner to stand down failed, and the truce turned against [[eleena-daru]] while the redirect began to recalculate.

How that standoff ended was never played out. The crew got clear of the core with the evidence, and what became of [[telsa-brie]], [[rusk]], [[arden-vel]], [[kalax]] and [[sera]] is still unknown.

## The Drop

The crew commandeered a freight platform and rode it down the station's spine toward a bribed berth at [[bay-nine]]. It stalled four decks above an Imperial checkpoint whose scan-tech had just tagged all five collar signatures, while sealing doors closed overhead.

[[vesh-talorr]] forced the manual brake release with a shriek of metal, and the platform came down on the checkpoint. The scan-tech and one trooper died outright; [[eleena-daru]] executed the second, pinned in the wreckage. The shriek brought a squad up behind them and triggered a lockdown bulkhead ahead.

[[eleena-daru]] forced the override with her [[code-cylinder]], [[kenno-thorn]] shielded [[vesh-talorr]] and returned fire, and [[vesh-talorr]] spent his last stun grenade covering the retreat. [[kenno-thorn]] held the doorway until all three were through, then sealed it on the squad. A dash down a service corridor, a ladder, and a corroded hatch that [[vesh-talorr]] forced open loudly brought them into a cargo bay within sight of [[bay-nine]].

## Capture

At the threshold, the [[cresh-spike]] final response fired across all five collars at once — no local trace, no console, no gunship required. The three dropped where they stood, joints locked, as Imperial personnel closed in from three directions. [[cassian-vorn]]'s voice came over a bulkhead speaker: *"Cute. Sit tight."*

Mission 01 ended in capture. 5 XP each.
