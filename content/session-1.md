## Before Vannix

[[eleena-daru]] and [[kenno-thorn]] began in Imperial custody at [[annex-17]]. A failed escape attempt and a vicious fight in the Cage Market against [[kalax]] and his two associates got them marked as dangerous prospects rather than ordinary prisoners. There they met the broker [[elo-kesh]], and [[eleena-daru]] kept a stolen [[code-cylinder]] hidden on her person throughout.

[[vesh-talorr]], a Duros pilot with an old grievance against [[eleena-daru]], was bound into the same fate. All three were collared, interrogated and handed to [[cassian-vorn]], an [[isb]] officer assembling deniable convict assets for filthy work.

Their first assignment: infiltrate [[vannix-station|Vannix Relay Station]], recover its central data core, erase its records, stage a pirate attack, and leave no witnesses alive. [[kalax]] and his two associates were sent in as a competing team.

## Collision Course

[[cassian-vorn]]'s insertion plan was brutally literal. The prisoners woke aboard the unmanned freighter [[mournful-credit]], locked on a collision course with the station's docking arm. [[vesh-talorr]] wrestled the approach into a more survivable maintenance collar, but the impact left him and [[eleena-daru]] badly hurt. [[kenno-thorn]] forced his way through the ruptured wreck and sealed the pressure breach.

## The Fight in the Wreck

Then [[kalax]]'s crew — [[kalax]], [[sera]] and a Nikto gunman — went for their weapons. [[kenno-thorn]] took the fight out of the Nikto. [[vesh-talorr]] shook off the crash, got his hands on a sidearm and wounded [[kalax]]. [[eleena-daru]] destroyed [[sera]]'s improvised melee weapon, then threw a frag grenade that killed [[cautious-nikto|the Nikto]] and wrecked some of the false-flag staging material with him. [[kenno-thorn]] took his pirate pistol.

The freighter's damaged transponder had already shaken hands with the station's systems. Security was on its way, and fire and suppression foam were eating what was left of the wreck.

[[kalax]] and [[sera]] broke off and escaped through a maintenance conduit the grenade had opened up. The crew grabbed what mission gear they could and followed. At a sealed service iris, [[eleena-daru]] tried to strike a deal with the pair. It failed: they went through, sealed it behind them, and left the crew's escape route exposed to the security teams coming up behind. The crew dropped into a downward utility crawlway with station forces closing in.

## The Evidence

The crew went for communications before the archives. In [[communications-control]] they found [[telsa-brie]] and a battered astromech called [[rusk]], trying to get proof of an Imperial massacre off the station. The crew froze the transmission at 91%, but [[rusk]] kept the cleanest copy. A security-droid response forced everyone onto an emergency route, and [[telsa-brie]] became the crew's guide — they were her best chance of keeping the [[vannix-evidence|evidence]] alive.

## The Alcove

In a side alcove, patching wounds, the crew made her talk. [[eleena-daru]] opened with an insult. [[vesh-talorr]]'s quieter question — what were you trying to do with it? — is what got an answer.

*"Cresh Spike is the restraint system. Collars, implants, remote compliance hardware. Batch-linked. Military prison stock, ISB-modified. It can stun you, drop you, track you, maybe kill you if the calibration is cruel enough."*

The archive held maintenance windows, authentication keys and collar-cart batch-sync data: enough to prove that prisoners were being used as disposable, deniable assets. Station Chief [[arden-vel]] had authorised getting it off-station after the killings started, and his aide had already contacted a Rebel relay cell. [[rusk]] carried the clean copy — "the one they didn't touch".

[[vesh-talorr]] asked whether she could get the collars off. [[eleena-daru]] reframed it: the crew would keep [[telsa-brie]] and [[rusk]] alive, but not while [[cassian-vorn]] could drop them with a button. [[telsa-brie]] tried a single diagnostic on [[vesh-talorr]]'s collar. It went badly — the collar threw a blue-white arc and convulsed him into the wall — but she learned something. The collars are batch-synced to a command node. With a calibration station, or a live technician console and officer-level keys, they might be able to spoof a maintenance cycle and perhaps disable the remote stun. *"Maybe."*

[[kenno-thorn]] spent the last stimpack on himself, then tried to patch [[eleena-daru]] while she flirted through the pain. *"Buy me a drink first next time."* When the dressing wouldn't hold, he cobbled half-spent injectors and clotting accelerant into one improvised dose for her.

[[telsa-brie]] named the [[central-computer-core]] as the place to try for the collars. [[kenno-thorn]] caught her guessing harder than she admitted. *"I'm not lying. I'm telling you where I'd look. That's different."* [[eleena-daru]] ended the argument by producing her [[code-cylinder]] from her prison smock, and [[telsa-brie]] dug out grey-blue station maintenance overalls for all three of them.

## The Core

[[telsa-brie]] led them up service ladders and a lift shaft, with [[rusk]] dragged along on a strip of insulation mat, until the passage opened above the [[central-computer-core]]: a vertical data spine, three decks of catwalks, and blue light pulsing through the conduits. Below were [[arden-vel]], wounded and holding a holdout blaster, two station-security survivors watching the lower doors, a pair of utility droids and an idle security droid. From above, [[kenno-thorn]] couldn't find a clean first strike.

It turned violent when [[eleena-daru]] took [[telsa-brie]] hostage. [[arden-vel]], both guards and the security droid went down, and a collapsing gantry buried the last guard and one of the doors. [[eleena-daru]] and [[vesh-talorr]] came out of it hurt and briefly out cold.

[[kalax]] and [[sera]] reappeared mid-fight and accepted a narrow ceasefire after [[eleena-daru]] offered them a path toward collar freedom. [[kalax]] opened a relay panel under the core; the [[black-warrant]] briefly identified all five collar-bearers, and [[telsa-brie]] forced its lock into a false maintenance loop.

## Eleven Minutes

Then the outer screen changed: Imperial assault shuttles on docking approach, minutes out. *"They are coming for you,"* [[telsa-brie]] said. *"And when they find me here, they will know exactly what I did."*

[[kalax]], cutter whining, wanted to follow orders — no witnesses. [[sera]] pointed out that [[telsa-brie]] had kept the loop alive through the fighting when she could have sold them all out. [[telsa-brie]] didn't plead. She could hide them in maintenance traffic, get the wounded out, and send the [[black-warrant]] to the wrong section of the station — *"But I will not walk willingly into a prison ship."*

[[eleena-daru]] told her to do it and redirect the gunship. Then she caught [[kenno-thorn]]'s and [[vesh-talorr]]'s eyes, drew a finger across her own throat, and drifted in behind the Mirialan with her blaster low.

[[sera]] saw it. Her weapon came up — at [[eleena-daru]]. *"Don't."* [[telsa-brie]] spun, understood everything, and froze with the redirect half-finished. *"I have eleven minutes. You shoot me, the loop dies, and that gunship comes straight back to this room."*

[[eleena-daru]] turned to [[kalax]] and told him to put a muzzle on his partner. For half a second it almost worked. Then [[sera]] stared her down, and [[kalax]] stepped back. *"You want the witness dead, smuggler? You wear it. Not me. Not her."* The truce had hardened against [[eleena-daru]]. At the terminal, [[telsa-brie]]'s hands had come off the keys, and an amber line crawled across the display: **BLACK WARRANT — VECTOR RECALCULATION DETECTED.**

How that standoff ended was never played out. The crew got clear of the core with the evidence, and what became of [[telsa-brie]], [[rusk]], [[arden-vel]], [[kalax]] and [[sera]] is still unknown.

## Which Way Out

On the run, the crew stopped long enough to argue a route. [[eleena-daru]] knew how dirty crews slip a lockdown: stations like this keep an unregistered service berth for contractors who don't want to be on the manifest — [[bay-nine]], low deck, aft, near the reclamation plant, with no transponder logging and an older docking-clamp control that doesn't answer to the central lockdown. [[kenno-thorn]] argued Imperial doctrine and the escape pods, confidently and possibly wrongly. [[vesh-talorr]] remembered that refit yards of this pattern have a central spine elevator running straight down to the docking ring.

They took the spine. The refit crews had welded off the passenger car but left the freight platform: a slab of durasteel grating in a shaft full of coolant machinery. It worked. It just wasn't built to hide anyone.

## The Drop

Four decks down, the platform stopped by itself above an Imperial checkpoint slotted into the shaft: two stormtroopers and a scan-tech in Imperial black running a collar sweep. *"...five signatures tagged off the Warrant. Grid says they're moving."* Sealing doors were closing overhead.

[[vesh-talorr]] forced the manual brake release with a shriek of metal, and the platform came down on the checkpoint. The scan-tech and one trooper died outright; [[eleena-daru]] executed the second, pinned in the wreckage. The shriek brought a squad up behind them and triggered a lockdown bulkhead ahead.

[[eleena-daru]] forced the override with her [[code-cylinder]], [[kenno-thorn]] shielded [[vesh-talorr]] and returned fire, and [[vesh-talorr]] spent his last stun grenade covering the retreat. [[kenno-thorn]] held the doorway until all three were through, then sealed it on the squad. A dash down a service corridor, a ladder, and a corroded hatch that [[vesh-talorr]] forced open loudly brought them into a cargo bay within sight of [[bay-nine]].

## Capture

At the threshold, the [[cresh-spike]] final response fired across all five collars at once — no local trace, no console, no gunship required. The three dropped where they stood, joints locked, as Imperial personnel closed in from three directions. [[cassian-vorn]]'s voice came over a bulkhead speaker: *"Cute. Sit tight."*

Mission 01 ended in capture. 5 XP each.
