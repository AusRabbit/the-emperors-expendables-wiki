The Imperial penal processing facility where [[eleena-daru]] and [[kenno-thorn]] began in custody, and where the crew's intake was overseen by [[lysa-venn]].

A failed escape attempt, and a vicious fight in the Cage Market against [[kalax]] and his two associates, marked the pair as dangerous prospects rather than ordinary prisoners. The broker [[elo-kesh]] worked the Cage Market, and [[eleena-daru]] kept a stolen [[code-cylinder]] hidden on her the whole time.

From here, the crew — now including [[vesh-talorr]] — were collared with [[cresh-spike]], interrogated, and handed to [[cassian-vorn]].
