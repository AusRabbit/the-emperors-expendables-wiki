## Session 1

- `M01` **Collared and deployed** — [[cassian-vorn]] loads collared prisoners aboard the [[mournful-credit]], staged as a pirate raider.
- `M01` **The crash** — The freighter hits [[vannix-station]]. [[kalax]] and [[sera]] become rivals; the [[cautious-nikto|Cautious Nikto]] dies to [[eleena-daru]]'s grenade and [[kenno-thorn]] takes his pistol.
- `M01` **Communications Control** — [[telsa-brie]] and [[rusk]] are caught moving proof of a massacre off-station. The transmission freezes at 91%; [[rusk]] keeps the clean copy.
- `M01` **What the collars are** — [[cresh-spike]] is revealed as ISB restraint tech with a sealed final response, relayed through the [[black-warrant]].
- `M01` **The Core** — [[eleena-daru]] takes [[telsa-brie]] hostage; [[arden-vel]] and the core's defenders fall. A ceasefire with [[kalax]] and [[sera]].
- `M01` **False loop** — The [[black-warrant]] identifies all five collars; [[telsa-brie]] loops its lock and starts a redirect.
- `M01` **The standoff** — [[eleena-daru]] moves to execute [[telsa-brie]]; [[sera]] draws on her. How it ended was never played out.
- `M01` **The spine** — A stolen freight platform stalls four decks above a checkpoint that has just tagged the collars.
- `M01` **The drop** — [[vesh-talorr]] releases the brake and the platform crushes the checkpoint. [[eleena-daru]] executes the survivor.
- `M01` **The bulkhead** — [[eleena-daru]]'s [[code-cylinder]] forces the lockdown; [[kenno-thorn]] holds the door until everyone is through.
- `M01` **Capture** — Within sight of [[bay-nine]], the final response drops all five collar-bearers. *"Cute. Sit tight."* 5 XP each.

## Session 2

- `INT` **Custody** — Cells on what feels like a moving [[detention-vessel|vessel]]. A medical team heals everyone before any questions.
- `INT` **Guard gossip** — [[eleena-daru]] gets a guard talking: silent running, cagey command, and "the *Rennick* run".
- `INT` **The debrief** — [[kalax]] and [[sera]] take the blame. [[kenno-thorn]]'s "Who's Telsa?" is caught out by a voiceprint.
- `INT` **Silence** — [[cassian-vorn]] reads the crew's silence as proof the [[vannix-evidence]] survived. "Later, and less politely."
- `INT` **Mission 02** — Kill [[doran-rennick]] and make it look like an accident. Not negotiable.
- `M02` **Redfall** — Landfall at [[redfall-station]] as a surveying and timber outfit. Relaxed soldiers, a bored customs check.
- `M02` **The cantina** — [[bant-corrl]] holds court at the [[redfall-cantina]]. The bartender tells [[vesh-talorr]] the town's peace has [[doran-rennick]]'s name on it.
- `M02` **The sweep** — [[eleena-daru]]'s [[forged-permits]] are flagged mid-flirt with [[bant-corrl]]. [[kenno-thorn]] staggers into the officer; [[vesh-talorr]] is caught eavesdropping.

## Session 3

- `M02` **The cantina breaks** — Three-sided brawl: sweep officers and loyal troopers, anti-Imperial locals, and the crew.
- `M02` **Two officers down** — [[vesh-talorr]] drops the first [[id-sweep-officers|officer]]; [[kenno-thorn]] kills the second.
- `M02` **Corrl turns** — [[bant-corrl]] commits against the crew. [[eleena-daru]]'s attempt to win him back fails.
- `M02` **Vesh falls** — [[bant-corrl]] shoots [[vesh-talorr]] twice in the doorway; the second shot leaves a serious wound.
- `M02` **Sirens** — Constabulary arrive. [[kenno-thorn]] carries [[vesh-talorr]] out and all three escape together.
- `M02` **Marn Oshel** — A backcountry local observes that the crew "doesn't fight like surveyors", and leaves. 5 XP each.
- `M02` **Wove at the door** — Morning at the [[loggers-house]]. [[ilse-wove]] asks for [[eleena-daru]] by name.
