The heart of [[vannix-station]]'s systems. [[telsa-brie]] gained access through [[arden-vel]]; [[eleena-daru]] turned it into a hostage situation. [[arden-vel]], two security survivors and the core's security droid went down, and a collapsed gantry buried one of the doorways.

It was here that [[kalax]] and [[sera]] took the ceasefire, [[kalax]] opened the relay panel beneath the core, and [[telsa-brie]] looped the [[black-warrant]]'s trace. It was also here that [[sera]] drew on [[eleena-daru]]. The crew got out with the evidence; how the standoff ended was never played.
