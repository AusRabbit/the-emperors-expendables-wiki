The heart of [[vannix-station]]'s systems: a wide vertical chamber built around a cylindrical data spine rising through three decks of grated catwalks, with blue status light pulsing through transparent conduits and maintenance arms hanging over the core. [[telsa-brie]] believed it was the place to try for the collars; she was more sure of that in her words than in her hands.

The crew came in from the upper maintenance access in stolen station overalls. Below were [[arden-vel]], two security survivors, two utility droids and an idle security droid. [[eleena-daru]] turned it into a hostage situation. [[arden-vel]], the guards and the security droid went down, and a collapsed gantry buried the last guard and one of the doorways.

It was here that [[kalax]] and [[sera]] took the ceasefire, [[kalax]] opened the relay panel beneath the core, and [[telsa-brie]] looped the [[black-warrant]]'s trace. It was also here that [[sera]] drew on [[eleena-daru]]. The crew got out with the evidence; how the standoff ended was never played.
