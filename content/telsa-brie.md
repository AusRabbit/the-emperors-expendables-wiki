A junior communications technician on [[vannix-station]]. When the crew found her in [[communications-control]], she and [[rusk]] were trying to transmit proof of an Imperial massacre off the station. The crew froze the transmission at 91%.

After that she became the crew's guide, because they were her best chance of keeping the [[vannix-evidence|evidence]] alive. It was her digging that exposed what [[cresh-spike]] really is. She got into the [[central-computer-core]] through [[arden-vel]], was taken hostage there by [[eleena-daru]], and still forced the [[black-warrant]]'s lock into a false loop and began redirecting it.

Then [[eleena-daru]] moved behind her to kill her, and [[sera]] drew on [[eleena-daru]] to stop it. How that standoff ended was never played out. At the debrief [[cassian-vorn]] asked about her directly, and [[kenno-thorn]]'s "Who's Telsa?" did not fool him.

Her whereabouts are unknown.
