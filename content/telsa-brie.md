A Mirialan junior communications technician on [[vannix-station]]. When the crew found her in [[communications-control]], she and [[rusk]] were trying to transmit proof of an Imperial massacre off the station. The crew froze the transmission at 91%.

After that she became the crew's guide, because they were her best chance of keeping the [[vannix-evidence|evidence]] alive. In a side alcove she told them what [[cresh-spike]] really is, what the archive held, and that Station Chief [[arden-vel]] had authorised the transmission after the killings started — his aide had already contacted a Rebel relay cell. *"I don't care what uniform they wear. They were the only ones outside Imperial command who might listen."*

She tried one diagnostic on [[vesh-talorr]]'s collar. It failed painfully, but it showed her the collars are batch-synced to a command node that might, with the right console and keys, be fooled. She pointed the crew at the [[central-computer-core]], more certain in her words than in her hands, and led them there.

In the Core she was taken hostage by [[eleena-daru]], still forced the [[black-warrant]]'s lock into a false loop, and offered to hide the crew and misdirect the gunship — *"But I will not walk willingly into a prison ship."* Then [[eleena-daru]] moved behind her to kill her, and [[sera]] drew on [[eleena-daru]] to stop it. How that standoff ended was never played out.

At the debrief [[cassian-vorn]] asked about her directly, and [[kenno-thorn]]'s "Who's Telsa?" did not fool him. Her whereabouts are unknown.
