## Somewhere Moving

The crew woke in cells along a narrow curved corridor: no windows, no chrono, and a deep thrum in the deck that never stopped. [[vesh-talorr]] felt the floor lean for half a second, which no station should do, and a distant metallic groan made the passing guard glance away like he had heard it before. Wherever this [[detention-vessel|holding facility]] was, it did not feel stationary.

An unseen medical team patched all three back to full health before anyone asked them a question.

[[eleena-daru]] charmed a guard into stopping at her cell and then into gossiping. He admitted the detail had been running silent for days with no rotations and that command was cagey even with its own people, and he let slip the name of the next job, "the *Rennick* run", once "this lot's processed". [[kenno-thorn]] found no weakness in his cell, but did find a reason to crouch out of the camera's view beside a floor grate that rang oddly hollow.

## The Debrief

Guards walked all three into a bare room to face [[cassian-vorn]], who wanted their account of Vannix.

They blamed [[kalax]] and [[sera]]. [[cassian-vorn]] was not convinced: his recovery teams had found no matching bodies, which meant the pair were either loose or the story was thinner than it looked. [[eleena-daru]] then gave a clean account of moving to eliminate [[telsa-brie]] as a loose end and [[sera]] stopping her, which fitted what he knew of [[sera]], and he let it stand.

When he asked about [[telsa-brie]], [[rusk]] and the [[vannix-evidence|evidence]], [[kenno-thorn]] tried "Who's Telsa?". [[cassian-vorn]] answered with his own recovery logs and a partial voiceprint naming all three of them, and marked the lie against them. Pressed again, the crew said nothing. He read the silence as confirmation that the evidence survived, rested two fingers on his collar-control cylinder without using it, and said the matter would be revisited "later, and less politely".

He asked whether they knew where they were. They gave him nothing, and he moved on.

Then he gave them Mission 02: kill [[doran-rennick]], garrison commander of [[cathals-reach]], and make it look like an accident, a rebel strike or a hunting mishap. [[doran-rennick]] had refused a reprisal massacre after a rebel ambush killed his soldiers, and hunted the actual cell himself instead. Command considers that restraint ideological contagion — proof that fear isn't needed to hold a world — and wants him erased before the example spreads. The order was not negotiable.

## Redfall

The crew landed at [[redfall-station]] under cover as an independent surveying, game and timber outfit: [[eleena-daru]] as its face, [[kenno-thorn]] as camp boss and [[vesh-talorr]] as pilot and logistics. A bored customs officer waved a scanner over their [[forged-permits]]. Nearby, two off-duty soldiers shared a thermos and a joke, more relaxed than garrison troops on an occupied world have any right to be.

The [[redfall-cantina]] was warm, loud and unafraid. Troopers drank with loggers, a card game ran hot in the corner, and [[bant-corrl]] held court at the longest table, buying rounds and plainly loved. [[kenno-thorn]]'s round at the bar earned nothing but a reason to linger. [[vesh-talorr]] did better: the bartender told him the town isn't happy, exactly, but isn't grieving either, and that the peace has a name on it — a commander who "actually looks at what's in front of him 'stead of just following the book".

## The Sweep

[[eleena-daru]] went to work on [[bant-corrl]] and had him charmed — right up until an Imperial ID sweep came in and flagged her permits as inconsistent with the spaceport manifest. An [[id-sweep-officers|officer]] took her by the arm, and [[bant-corrl]]'s warmth went cold.

[[kenno-thorn]], three drinks in, staggered into the officer just as he reached for his comlink and bought a few seconds of confusion. [[vesh-talorr]], trying to eavesdrop, was caught at it, and a second officer fixed his attention on him and kept it there.

The session ended with [[eleena-daru]] in custody's grip, [[vesh-talorr]] marked, and [[bant-corrl]] watching in silence.
