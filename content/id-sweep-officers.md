Two unnamed Imperial officers running an identity sweep through the [[redfall-cantina]].

The first flagged [[eleena-daru]]'s [[forged-permits]] as inconsistent with the spaceport manifest and took her by the arm. [[kenno-thorn]]'s drunken stagger knocked him off his comlink before he could call for backup. When he tried to drag her out, the cantina erupted. He shot [[eleena-daru]] on stun, [[vesh-talorr]] dropped him, and the crew took a binder-release key and a spare charge pack off him. He did not survive the night's fighting.

The second caught [[vesh-talorr]] eavesdropping and never took his eyes off him again. [[kenno-thorn]] killed him with a three-shot burst, which is what turned [[bant-corrl]] against the crew.
