The chief of [[vannix-station]]. After the killings started, he authorised getting the [[vannix-evidence|evidence]] off the station, and his aide had already made contact with a Rebel relay cell.

The crew found him in the [[central-computer-core]]: middle-aged, in a stained grey uniform, one eye swollen nearly shut, a holdout blaster in his hand, with two security survivors holding the lower doors. Through him, [[telsa-brie]] got provisional access to the Core. The confrontation there turned violent and he was incapacitated.

His status since the crew left the Core is unknown.
