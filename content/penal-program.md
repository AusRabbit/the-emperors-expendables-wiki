The programme that owns the crew, run by [[isb]] officers like [[cassian-vorn]]. Prisoners from places like [[annex-17]] are fitted with [[cresh-spike]] collars and deployed on missions the Empire cannot be seen to run. Rival teams can be sent in together, as [[kalax]]'s crew were on Vannix. Unofficially, its assets are known as the Emperor's Dirty Dozen.

The [[vannix-evidence]] includes proof that the programme exists.

So far it has sent the crew to [[vannix-station]] and to [[cathals-reach]].
