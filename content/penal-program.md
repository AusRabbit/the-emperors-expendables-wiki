The programme that owns the crew. Prisoners are fitted with [[cresh-spike]] collars and deployed on missions the Empire cannot be seen to run, under handlers like [[cassian-vorn]]. Unofficially, its assets are known as the Emperor's Dirty Dozen.

So far it has sent the crew to [[vannix-station]] and to [[cathals-reach]].
