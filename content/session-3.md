## Three Sides

The [[id-sweep-officers|officer]] holding [[eleena-daru]] ran out of patience and tried to drag her out, and the [[redfall-cantina]] came apart. The two sweep officers and a table of loyal troopers took one side, a knot of anti-Imperial locals waded in on the other, and the crew were caught between them. [[bant-corrl]] held back from both.

[[kenno-thorn]] fired on the officer holding [[eleena-daru]] and missed, but opened a lane. [[eleena-daru]] tried a knee strike behind a damsel-in-distress act; it did not land, but the act was good enough to pull a sympathiser to her side. The officer shot her point-blank on stun. [[vesh-talorr]] shot him in turn and dropped him.

[[kenno-thorn]] turned on the second officer and killed him with a cold, precise three-shot burst. Watching his own people die, [[bant-corrl]] finally chose a side, and it was not the crew's. [[eleena-daru]]'s desperate attempt to win him back failed badly.

## Corrl's Shots

Loyalist reinforcements arrived and broke the locals. The crew looted a binder-release key and a spare charge pack off the first officer, and [[vesh-talorr]] broke for the exit, laying down suppressing fire to cover the others.

[[bant-corrl]] shot him anyway — once, blind and precise, as he reached the doorway, and then again. The second shot put [[vesh-talorr]] down in the doorway with a serious wound.

Constabulary sirens ended it. The locals who could still move scattered, and the troopers fell back around [[bant-corrl]] rather than give chase. [[kenno-thorn]] hauled [[vesh-talorr]] out through a side passage, and all three got away into the dark together.

## The Logger's House

In the confusion a backcountry local, [[marn-oshel]], was briefly there. He remarked flatly that the crew "doesn't fight like surveyors", ignored [[kenno-thorn]]'s attempt to draw him out, and walked off into the night.

The crew went to ground in a borrowed [[loggers-house]] at the edge of [[redfall-station]]. Overnight they pulled [[vesh-talorr]] back from the edge, though his wound is still untreated, and [[eleena-daru]] and [[kenno-thorn]] got their breath back.

Encounter award: 5 XP each.

## Wove at the Door

Grey morning light. [[ilse-wove]], [[doran-rennick]]'s second, walked up to the house in person with two troopers and asked to speak to [[eleena-daru]] — by name, and alone. She has not drawn a weapon or made a threat. She is standing at the door, hands clasped, waiting.

Nobody has answered it yet.
