A battered astromech working with [[telsa-brie]] in [[communications-control]]. When the crew froze the outgoing transmission, Rusk kept the cleanest copy of the [[vannix-evidence|massacre evidence]] — "the one they didn't touch", as [[telsa-brie]] put it.

Too damaged to move itself, it was dragged to the [[central-computer-core]] on a strip of insulation mat, offering the occasional broken chirp. Its whereabouts and condition since then are unknown. [[cassian-vorn]] asked about it by name.
