Imperial Army garrison commander of [[cathals-reach]], and the man [[cassian-vorn]] has ordered the crew to kill.

## Why He's a Target

After a rebel ambush killed several of his soldiers, Rennick was expected to carry out a doctrinal reprisal against the local population. He refused. He hunted down the cell responsible himself and left the town standing. His garrison has been unusually loyal and slow to desert ever since.

Command reads that as ideological contagion: living proof that fear isn't needed to hold a world. It wants him quietly erased before the idea spreads — by an accident, a rebel strike or a hunting mishap, nothing traceable.

## What the Crew Have Heard

The bartender at the [[redfall-cantina]] told [[vesh-talorr]] that the town's peace has Rennick's name on it — a commander who "actually looks at what's in front of him 'stead of just following the book". Before that, a guard in custody let slip that the crew's next job was "the *Rennick* run".

## Kenno

[[kenno-thorn]] served with him years ago, before his own fall. Rennick was the officer Kenno measured "doing it right" against. They have not met again yet, and whether Rennick will recognise him is an open question.

His second is [[ilse-wove]].
