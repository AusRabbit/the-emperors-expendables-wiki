Forged surveying and outfitter permits supplied for the crew's cover on [[cathals-reach]]. They passed a bored customs check at [[redfall-station]], but an ID sweep at the [[redfall-cantina]] flagged [[eleena-daru]]'s set as inconsistent with the spaceport manifest. The officer who took them did not survive the brawl. Nobody checked [[kenno-thorn]]'s or [[vesh-talorr]]'s.
