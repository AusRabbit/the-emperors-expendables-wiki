The collars every member of the crew wears. What they are came out on [[vannix-station]], through [[telsa-brie]]'s digging.

## What They Do

Cresh Spike is an ISB-modified prisoner-restraint system: tracking, forced compliance, remote stun, and a sealed "final response". The collars are batch-linked to a remote command structure. A station like Vannix can mirror and route their traffic, but cannot remove or control them.

A maintenance handshake can forge a limited status update or interfere with future synchronisation. It cannot take a collar off.

## The Relay

The true command relay during Mission 01 was the gunship [[black-warrant]], whose trace [[telsa-brie]] looped. It made no difference: at the threshold of [[bay-nine]], the final response dropped all five collar-bearers at once, independent of any local trace, console or the gunship. [[cassian-vorn]] holds the authority behind it, and carries a collar-control cylinder.

## What the Crew Have Noticed

- [[kenno-thorn]], during the fitting: a magnetic latch, a receiver node at the nape, and a recessed technician's service port.
- [[vesh-talorr]]: a batch-synchronisation window in how the collars are handled — a possible weakness in the procedure.

Where the root controller is, and whether the final response can be blocked or reversed, are still [[threads|open questions]].
