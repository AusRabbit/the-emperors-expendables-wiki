Senior NCO of the [[cathals-reach-garrison]]. Warm, loud and generous, he runs the culture of the [[redfall-cantina]] and seems to know every trooper by name. The crew's first sight of him was at the head of the longest table, buying rounds, loved rather than merely obeyed.

[[eleena-daru]] had him charmed until the ID sweep came in. The moment uniforms were involved, his warmth went cold, and he held back from the fight until [[kenno-thorn]] killed the second [[id-sweep-officers|sweep officer]] in front of him. Then he committed against the crew, and [[eleena-daru]]'s attempt to win him back failed.

He shot [[vesh-talorr]] twice in the doorway, the second shot leaving a serious wound. When the constabulary arrived he held the scene rather than give chase.

He is now a hostile eyewitness with every reason to name the crew. Whatever he felt for [[eleena-daru]] is probably gone for good.
