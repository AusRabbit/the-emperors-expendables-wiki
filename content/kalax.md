One of the other collared prisoners crashed into [[vannix-station]] with the crew, and a competitor from the moment the [[mournful-credit]] hit. He and his partner [[sera]] reappeared in the [[central-computer-core]] and accepted a narrow ceasefire when [[eleena-daru]] offered them a path toward collar freedom. Kalax opened the relay panel that let [[telsa-brie]] loop the [[black-warrant]]'s trace.

When [[sera]] drew on [[eleena-daru]], he refused to turn a weapon on his own partner, and the truce hardened against the crew. He counts [[kenno-thorn]] and [[eleena-daru]] as enemies.

At the debrief the crew blamed him for the mission's failures. [[cassian-vorn]]'s people found no body. His fate is unconfirmed.
