A fellow prisoner and a rival from the start. He and his two associates, [[sera]] and a Nikto gunman, fought [[eleena-daru]] and [[kenno-thorn]] in the Cage Market at [[annex-17]], and [[cassian-vorn]] sent all three into [[vannix-station]] as a competing team.

They went for weapons in the wreck of the [[mournful-credit]]. [[vesh-talorr]] wounded Kalax and [[cautious-nikto|the Nikto]] died, and Kalax and [[sera]] slipped away through a maintenance conduit. When [[eleena-daru]] tried to strike a deal at a sealed service iris, they went through it, sealed it, and left the crew exposed.

They reappeared in the [[central-computer-core]] and took a ceasefire when [[eleena-daru]] offered them a path toward collar freedom. Kalax opened the relay panel that let [[telsa-brie]] loop the [[black-warrant]]'s trace. With a plasma cutter in hand, he wanted [[telsa-brie]] dead on Vorn's orders — but when [[eleena-daru]] tried to make him turn on [[sera]], he stepped back. *"You want the witness dead, smuggler? You wear it. Not me. Not her."* He counts [[kenno-thorn]] and [[eleena-daru]] as enemies.

At the debrief the crew blamed him for the mission's failures. [[cassian-vorn]]'s people found no body. His fate is unconfirmed.
