The spaceport and logging town on [[cathals-reach]] where the crew landed, posing as an independent surveying, game and timber outfit. Their cover gear is stored in a rented shed near the spaceport.

On arrival, a bored customs officer barely glanced at their [[forged-permits]], and two off-duty soldiers shared a thermos and a joke nearby — a first glimpse of how unusually calm the place is.

The calm did not survive the crew. After the brawl at the [[redfall-cantina]], the streets have the hush of a town waiting to see who gets blamed. The crew are lying low in a [[loggers-house]] on the edge of town.
