A matte-black, officer-grade Imperial code cylinder that [[eleena-daru]] stole and kept hidden on her person through her custody at [[annex-17]]. [[elo-kesh]] knows it exists.

On [[vannix-station]] she finally produced it — from the slit of her prison smock — to convince [[telsa-brie]] the crew could get a restricted console in the [[central-computer-core]] to accept them. Later she used it openly to force a lockdown bulkhead during the escape toward [[bay-nine]].

Whether it survived the capture hasn't been settled.
