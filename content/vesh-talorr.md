A Corellian-born Duros pilot contracted to [[kordyne]]. Calm in the cockpit, precise under pressure, and ambitious enough to treat every engagement as another line in the legend he means to have. His signature fighter, the [[sure-thing]], is long gone — reassigned or impounded by [[kordyne]] well before any of this, and still a sore point.

## History

- Recognised [[eleena-daru]] from a previous betrayal.
- [[kordyne]] holds his contract debt.
- Spotted a batch-synchronisation window in how the [[cresh-spike]] collars are handled — a possible weakness in the procedure.

## On Vannix

Forced the freight platform's manual brake and brought it down on the checkpoint. Spent his last stun grenade covering the retreat, and forced the corroded hatch that led toward [[bay-nine]]. In custody he felt the floor lean when no floor should.

## On Cathal's Reach

Pilot and logistics for the surveying outfit. Got the bartender at the [[redfall-cantina]] talking about [[doran-rennick]], then got caught eavesdropping. In the brawl he dropped the first [[id-sweep-officers|sweep officer]] and covered the escape with suppressing fire, until [[bant-corrl]] shot him twice in the doorway.

He is recovering at the [[loggers-house]] with an untreated wound, not fit to fight.
