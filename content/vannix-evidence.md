The archive [[telsa-brie]] and [[rusk]] were trying to get off [[vannix-station]]. It holds proof of the Imperial killings on the station — the thing the "pirate raid" was staged to bury — along with [[cresh-spike]] maintenance windows, authentication keys and collar-cart batch-sync data. That is enough to prove prisoners are being used as disposable, deniable assets: the crew's own [[penal-program]].

Station Chief [[arden-vel]] authorised the transmission after the killings started, and his aide had already reached a Rebel relay cell. The crew froze the transmission at 91%. [[rusk]] kept the clean copy — "the one they didn't touch".

The crew got clear of the [[central-computer-core]] with the evidence. At the debrief, [[cassian-vorn]] asked whether it survived; the crew said nothing, and he took the silence as a yes. He said he would come back to the matter "later, and less politely".

Its status since the capture hasn't been settled at the table.
