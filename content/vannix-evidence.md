Proof of an Imperial massacre on [[vannix-station]] — the thing the "pirate raid" was staged to bury. [[telsa-brie]] and [[rusk]] were transmitting it off-station when the crew froze the transmission at 91%. [[rusk]] kept the cleanest copy.

The crew got clear of the [[central-computer-core]] with the evidence. At the debrief, [[cassian-vorn]] asked whether it survived; the crew said nothing, and he took the silence as a yes. He said he'd assume someone is carrying it, and that he would come back to the matter "later, and less politely".

Its status since the capture hasn't been settled at the table.
