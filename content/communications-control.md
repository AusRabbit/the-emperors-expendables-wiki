The communications hub of [[vannix-station]]. The crew went for communications before the archives, and found [[telsa-brie]] and [[rusk]] part-way through transmitting proof of a massacre off the station. The crew froze the transmission at 91%, and [[rusk]] kept the cleanest copy. A security-droid response drove everyone out onto an emergency route.
