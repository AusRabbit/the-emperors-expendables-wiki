The Imperial Army garrison on [[cathals-reach]], commanded by [[doran-rennick]] with [[ilse-wove]] as his second and [[bant-corrl]] as the NCO who holds the ranks together. Loyalty is high and desertion low, and the reason is Rennick's refusal to massacre the town. That loyalty to him personally is the biggest obstacle to the crew's mission.

Its off-duty troopers drink with the locals at the [[redfall-cantina]]. After [[session-3]], two Imperial officers are dead and the crew are the ones being looked for.
